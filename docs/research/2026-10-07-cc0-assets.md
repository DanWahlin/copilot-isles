# CC0 assets for Copilot Isles

2026-10-07. CC0 equivalents for the gaps found in `2026-10-07-free-unity-assets.md`. Every entry was
license-checked on its source page (the CC0 statement or a `creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/`
link). CC0 assets can be committed to a public repo. Log each one in `ASSETS.md` when it's used.

## Top recommendations (current islands first)

1. **[Quaternius Fantasy Props MegaKit](https://quaternius.com/packs/fantasypropsmegakit.html)** (211 models, glTF): chests, market stalls, barrels, tools. Anchorage docks and bard's camp, Meridian plaza stalls. Same style as our kits.
2. **[Quaternius Ships Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ships.html)** (6 models, FBX/OBJ): rowboats and sloops for both harbours and the future Keelhaven shipyard.
3. **[KayKit Dungeon Pack](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-dungeon-pack)** (200+, glTF, one 1024 atlas): crates, barrels, sacks, ropes, tavern bar. Dock cargo and the captain's cabin.
4. **[Kenney Pirate Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/pirate-kit)** (70, glTF): cannons, cannonballs, flags, wheel, mast, chest. Sea battles and docks. A little blockier than Quaternius.
5. **[Quaternius Ultimate Modular Ruins](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularruins.html)** (90): fortress masonry for Meridian's citadel and ruins on the headland.
6. **[Quaternius Modular Medieval Buildings](https://quaternius.com/packs/modularmedievalbuildings.html)** (30): pieces for Meridian's terraced houses.
7. **[KayKit Medieval Hexagon](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-medieval-hexagon)** (200+): mill, market, well, tower buildings in four palettes. Meridian's plaza and the future guild town.
8. **[Quaternius Ultimate House Interior](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatehomeinterior.html)** (123) and **[Ultimate Furniture](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatefurniture.html)** (20): desk, shelves and cabinets for the captain's cabin.
9. **[Quaternius Ultimate Animated Animal Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html)** (12 animals, glTF, animated): island wildlife.
10. **[Quaternius Steampunk Turret Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/turretpack.html)** (37) and **[Modular Weapons](https://quaternius.com/packs/medievalweapons.html)** (24): cannon variety for raiders, and clockwork flavour for the caldera island.
11. **[KayKit Resource Bits](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/resource-bits)** (75+): stacks of wood, stone, iron, gold and cloth for docks and the shipyard.
12. **[Quaternius Ultimate Nature Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatenature.html)** (150) and **[Stylized Tree Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizedtree.html)** (45): more broadleaf variety now, and cacti and palms for the future desert island.

### Textured Fantasy Nature (#17)

Quaternius, March 2017, CC0 (page: "License CC0"). 11 models: fantasy trees, flowers, bushes and
mushrooms, **textured with gradients** (one small gradient texture shared by all models), in FBX, OBJ
and Blend (no glTF; convert in Blender). One shared gradient texture means one material and one
pipeline for the whole set, and recolouring per island is a texture or TSL tint swap. It's small, so
it complements a larger set (Stylized Tree Pack, KayKit Forest) rather than covering an island alone.
Plan: add it as vegetation species with the island's own `IslandVegetationRules` (the next island
after Anchorage Cay is Port Meridian, which is built; the first new island is the next candidate).
