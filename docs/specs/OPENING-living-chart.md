# Spec: The opening — the Captain's Chest and the living chart

> Dan chose this on 2026-10-04 (option "Chest + living chart"). Our own idea, not a copy of A World
> Within's ship-in-a-bottle. Classic Disney films open on a storybook coming to life; ours opens on the
> Captain's Chart, the map players already use to travel.

## The beat (about 20–30 s, skippable at any time with E, Space, Esc or a click)

1. **The cabin.** A warm, lamp-lit captain's cabin at golden hour: a heavy oak sea chest on the desk,
   a lantern swaying gently, dust motes in a shaft of light through the stern window, the creak of
   timbers and soft waves outside. The title and menu sit in the left third, as today.
2. **The chest.** On **New Voyage** (or **Continue**), the camera eases in. The latch snaps, the lid
   anticipates (a small dip) and swings open with a puff of dust and a warm glow from inside.
3. **The chart.** A rolled parchment chart rises from the chest and unrolls across the desk (follow-
   through on the paper's curl and settle). It's the Captain's Chart in our ink-and-parchment style:
   Anchorage Cay, Port Meridian, fogged islands, a compass rose, the route line.
4. **It comes alive.** The inked sea ripples and turns to real water, from the centre outward. The
   islands rise from the paper like a pop-up book (Anchorage first, its palms unfolding), and a tiny
   brigantine on the chart catches the wind.
5. **The dive.** The camera swoops down into the chart and through the surface of the sea (a soft
   white flash of spray), and lands on the real world: Anchorage's arrival flyover (or, on Continue,
   wherever the save left off). The music swells from the cabin theme into the island's theme.

## Rules

- **One world, not two.** The miniature islands on the chart are the real island meshes (or their
  impostors) at small scale, so they always match the game. No second art set.
- **Budgets.** The cabin is a small set (one room, ~15 props) built from our kits plus a few hero props
  (chest, chart, lantern). It loads with the title chunk within the §13.5 title budget, and the island
  pack streams during the dive (it's the loading screen in disguise).
- **Accessibility.** Reduced motion: a short crossfade from the open chest to the island card instead
  of the dive. Skippable every time; after the first viewing, Continue skips straight to the dive.
- **Disney bar.** Anticipation and follow-through on the latch, the lid and the paper; warm key light
  with cool fill; no hard cuts except the spray flash; the camera always moves on eased arcs.

## Later uses of the same pieces

- Opening the chest again from the pause menu shows the chart (fast travel, already in the game).
- Each new island's first arrival can rise from the chart the same way, in a few seconds.
